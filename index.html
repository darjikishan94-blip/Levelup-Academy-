Our approach

Teach deeply. Test honestly. Fix what's weak.

Our goal is for an average student to reach full marks in school exams while building real conceptual understanding.

📝

Regular chapter tests

Short tests of 20–50 marks after every chapter, with MCQ, assertion–reason, short, long and case-based questions.

🏛️

Board-style exams

80-mark half-yearly papers set in the board pattern, mixing board-level questions with deeper conceptual and competency-based ones.

✅

Strict, fair marking

Every paper has a step-wise marking scheme. Correct alternative methods get full credit, and one early slip is never penalised twice.

📊

Detailed evaluation reports

Marks by section, by chapter and by question type, plus a one-page improvement report: what you did well and your biggest mistakes.

🎯

Diagnostic workbooks

When a student struggles, we rank the root causes and prescribe a personal remedial workbook: warm-up, guided practice, then a mastery check.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parents kept informed

Class WhatsApp groups for parents, and parent summaries explaining what was learned and how to help at home.

FAQ

Questions parents often ask

Which classes and boards do you teach?

Classes 8 to 12. We teach students from both CBSE and GSEB schools in Gujarat, following the NCERT textbooks, including the old NCERT syllabus where a school still uses it.

Which subjects are covered?

Science for Classes 8 to 10 (Biology, Physics and Chemistry) and Physics for Classes 11 and 12.

Do you prepare students for GUJCET?

Yes. Class 12 students take full-syllabus GUJCET Physics mock tests with complete worked solutions.

How often are tests held?

Chapter tests run throughout the year, and there is a full 80-mark half-yearly examination in the board pattern. Every test comes with an answer key and marking scheme.

When does the academic year start, and how are fees paid?

The academic year runs from April to March. Fees are collected quarterly: April–June, July–September, October–December and January–March. Please contact us for the fee for your child's class.

How will I know how my child is doing?

After tests, students receive an evaluation report showing marks by section and chapter, what they did well and their biggest mistakes. Parents are kept updated through the class WhatsApp group.
