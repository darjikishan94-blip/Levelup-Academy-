FAQ

Questions parents often ask

Which boards do you cover?

We cover CBSE, ICSE and State Board syllabi. Batches are grouped by board and grade.

Are classes online or offline?

Both. You can attend at our centre or join live online classes. Recordings are available for missed sessions.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. The first demo class and the diagnostic assessment are completely free, with no obligation to enrol.

How are fees paid?

Fees can be paid monthly, quarterly or yearly. Quarterly and yearly plans include a discount. Contact us for the current fee structure.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Parents receive a monthly report with attendance, test scores and teacher remarks, plus a parent–teacher meeting every term.
